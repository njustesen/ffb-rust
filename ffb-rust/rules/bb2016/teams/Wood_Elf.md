# Wood Elf

```
WOOD ELF TEAMS
For Wood Elves the Long pass is everything, even more so than their High Elf cousins, and all of their
effort goes into being an expert at throwing or receiving. No Wood Elf worth his salt is going to be
weighed down by extra Armour and be forced to lurk about, attempting to knock opposing players
over. Instead they rely on their natural athletic ability to keep them out of trouble, which is normally
enough – it takes a very agile or lucky opponent to lay a hand on a Wood Elf!
```
