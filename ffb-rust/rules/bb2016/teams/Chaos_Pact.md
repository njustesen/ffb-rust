```
CHAOS PACT
Chaos Pact teams are a mix of evil and chaotic races. Human Marauders are young on the path of Chaos
and full of potential, but must be guided to fill the needs of the team. With such a mix of races animosity
can easily become a problem and it is rare to see a well organized and effective Chaos Pact team.
```
