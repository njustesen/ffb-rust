Why it was needed

A checker calibrated against its own subject

The squads were checked against a list of conditions in the project’s own requirements document, and that list was written from the squads. The money formula said so in as many words — derived from the data, not assumed — and offered as evidence that it reproduces the declared team value / spent / treasury for 100% of the squads in the tree. It reproduced them because the squads had been built with it. Nothing in the document required a squad to be legal under the actual Blood Bowl drafting rules, so these seven things were all reported as clean:

The rule that was missing

R6 — legal under the rulebook, per play format

A 1,100,000 budget is not a League budget; League is 1,000,000. It is a Matched Play budget for BB2025 — the most common values are 1,100,000, 1,150,000 or 1,200,000 — and an Exhibition Play budget for BB2020, whose range is 1,100,000 and 1,300,000. The drafting rules differ by format, and the squads had been following neither. Every constant below now traces to a quoted line in rules/, and none to a value read out of the squads.

How each squad was bought

Purchase order

Players first, then re-rolls, then the rest — as specified. The shaded steps are the ones where the order was left to me, and each says what it cost to decide the other way.

  1. One of every positional the caps allowAlso what satisfies the requirement that every position’s stat line and starting skills get exercised.
  2. More players, dearest firstWhile the squad can still reach 11 bodies, 3 re-rolls and an apothecary afterwards. This is the step that makes them look like teams.
  3. Cheapest player up to 11, then 12 where it costs no re-roll11 is the rule. The 12th is a preference: a squad on exactly 11 plays a man short after one casualty.
  4. Two team re-rolls
  5. Apothecary, then a third re-rollMy call, inside your “more players or staff”. Buying the third re-roll first left 57 of 94 eligible rosters with no apothecary — and took most of the apothecary mechanic’s exercise off the matrix.
  6. A 13th body, then one more dearest-first passSwaps a cheap body for the dearest positional that still has room, wherever the remaining gold covers the difference.
  7. Fans to the drafting maximum
  8. Assistant coaches to 6, then cheerleaders to 6The rulebook’s own advice for leftover cash, and the first time any parity squad has had a single member of staff.
  9. Players to 16, then re-rolls to 8Whatever the 10,000-gold staff could not absorb. What still cannot be spent is lost — and the drafter proves it by checking that nothing cheaper remains buyable.

My reading of them

Are these good teams?

Legality is machine-checked; whether they are teams a coach would field is a judgement, so here it is plainly.

Mostly yes, and the compositions are the evidence

Chaos Chosen takes 4 Chosen Blockers, a Minotaur and 7 Beastmen. Khemri takes 4 Tomb Guardians, 2 Blitz-ras, 2 Thro-ras and 4 Skeletons. Humans take an Ogre, 2 Blitzers, 2 Catchers, 2 Throwers and a Halfling Hopeful. Those are the builds these rosters are known for, and they came out of the dearest-first rule rather than being written down by hand.

26 squads sit on exactly 11 players, and that is the right call

They are the expensive rosters — the elf family, Dwarf, Chaos Renegades, Slann. Fielding one of every positional costs 450–520k there, and after 3 re-rolls and an apothecary there is no 12th body to be had. A tournament coach makes the same trade: 11 players with three re-rolls and an apothecary beats 12 without. The one squad that also loses its apothecary is bb2016 Chaos Renegades, whose ten positionals include four Big Guys and cost 895k on their own.

Only 12 squads hired any Sideline Staff

Players come before staff in the order you set, and on most rosters the players eat the budget. So the assistant-coach and cheerleader terms in Brilliant Coaching and Cheering Fans are exercised in 12 of 110 cells rather than most of them. That is still 12 more than before, where the fields were hardcoded to zero and those two kick-off results were decided 0-against-0 in all 33,300 games ever measured — but it is a modest gain, and reversing the order would trade squad quality for it.

180,000 gold is still lost, and a smarter drafter would lose less

24 squads cannot spend their last coins, 21 of them stuck on exactly 5,000 — because in bb2016 and bb2020 nothing on the sheet costs less than 10,000, so a 5,000 remainder has no home. That is the rule working (unspent gold is lost, and the drafter proves nothing cheaper remains buyable), not a bug. But it is greedy, not exact: a search that chose a 75,000 player over a 70,000 one in the right place would land on zero. It is 0.15% of the 121,000,000 drafted, and I left it.

One cell lost its second squad

bb2020 Chaos Pact had a renegadetroll variant, drafted to work around a group Big Guy cap. That cap comes from the official team page, and Chaos Pact has no official BB2020 page — it is a FUMBBL legacy import, governed by its per-position limits alone. So the base squad now fields all four of its Big Guys itself, the variant became a byte-for-byte duplicate, and I deleted it. The matrix is 110 cells and 330 gates, not 111 and 333; no positional lost coverage.

The cost

What the star removal takes off the pitch

Those 16 stars were the only players in the whole matrix carrying these, and each one was driven green by its own campaign. With no stars, they are exercised by unit tests and nothing else:

  • Hail Mary Pass under bb2016 — no roster in that ruleset has a native carrier, so it goes fully dark there. BB2025 keeps it: the Elven Union Thrower has it printed.
  • Treacherous, Black Ink, Then I Started Blastin’, Raiding Party, Look Into My Eyes, Baleful Hex, Catch of the Day — the whole star-special family, which rides one dispatch channel.
  • All You Can Eat, Wisdom of the White Dwarf, Multiple Block, Old Pro on Helmut Wulf.

Getting them back means hiring stars the way the rules do it — through inducements, paying gold and Skill Points against a team’s Tier allowance — which is a mechanic neither engine implements today, not a data change.
